Everything a sticky note should do.

Quick to grab, out of your way, and right where you left it.

🗒️

Notes on your desktop

As many notes as you like, in eight easy-to-read colors. Float them on top, make them translucent, or collapse them to a strip.

☑

Clickable checklists

Click a box to tick it. Each note shows its progress, like 3/5, in its header.

🔔

Reminders

Pick a time and NoteIt sends a notification that opens the note.

📚

All Notes, organized

Notebooks, #tags, favorites, archive, and trash, in one window with fast search.

⌨️

Quick capture

Press ⌃⌥N in any app for a new note, or make one from selected text through the Services menu.

☁️

Sync and backups

Sign in to sync your notes across Macs. NoteIt also keeps a daily backup you can restore.

AI that edits, not chats.

Click ✦ on a note and NoteIt rewrites it in place. Undo puts the original back.

One-click actions

Built-in actions for everyday notes, and you can write your own.

  • Fix Spelling & Grammar
  • Make Shorter
  • Make Longer
  • Summarize
  • Make a List
  • Make a Checklist
  • Professional Tone
  • Friendly Tone
  • Translate

Your provider, your key

Use Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible service. Prefer to stay offline? Point it at Ollama or LM Studio on your own Mac. API keys stay in the macOS Keychain.

Install NoteIt.

The quickest way is Homebrew. It also keeps NoteIt up to date.

  1. Open Terminal and run:
    $ brew install --cask its-ash/tap/noteit
  2. Find the NoteIt icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen. NoteIt has no Dock icon.
  3. To update later, run brew upgrade --cask noteit.

Prefer a direct download? Get the zip from the latest release and drag NoteIt into Applications.
